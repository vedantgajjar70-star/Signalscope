02 — what it's built to survive

Detection that holds up outside the lab

Most detectors ace the generator they trained on and fail on the next one. This is built for the next one.

Real vs. AI-generatedCore classification
A single label and a calibrated confidence score for any input image — scenes, objects, art, or product shots.
Unseen-generator testingGeneralisation, weighted highest
Held-out evaluation includes generators the model never trained on — the scenario that actually matters once new models ship.
Calibrated uncertainty"Not sure" is an honest output
Confidence reflects real reliability, not raw model score — so a borderline image reads as borderline, not as false certainty.
Robust to degradationCompression, resizing, screenshots
Accuracy is tracked as images are re-compressed, resized, or screenshotted — the real-world conditions detectors usually break on.
